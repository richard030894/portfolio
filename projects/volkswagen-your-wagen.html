Your Wagen

Volkswagen · Social · 2026

The second iteration of Volkswagen’s successful campaign “YourWagen”. A beautiful way of celebrating authentic stories showcasing how the Volkswagen brand has touched their lives. Directed by the wonderful Tom Cockram and edited by Sara Faulkner, this campaign follows four people who each tell their stories on how the brand has been a large part of their lives.

I worked across all the post-production elements as part of this campaign, including edit, grade, sound and digital deliverables.
